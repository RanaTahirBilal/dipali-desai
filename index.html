About Dr. Desai

A family doctor who looks at the whole person

Dipali A. Desai, MD, is a board certified family physician who cares for adult and geriatric patients in both hospital and office settings. Her work centres on the long conditions that shape daily life: diabetes, congestive heart failure and high blood pressure, along with routine physicals and preventive wellness.

She is also certified in integrative psychiatry by the Integrative Psychiatry Institute, and she brings that training into ordinary family medicine, so that a patient's mental health is treated alongside the physical picture rather than separately from it.

She practises at UH Southwest Internal Medicine in Middleburg Heights, Ohio, and speaks English, Gujarati and Hindi.

Care

What she looks after

Family medicine for adults and older patients, in the office and on the ward.

Long-term conditions

Diabetes, congestive heart failure and high blood pressure, followed over time in the office and in hospital.

Physicals and prevention

Routine physicals and preventive wellness, with specialty testing where it helps to explain what is going on.

Integrative psychiatry

Mental health considered as part of the whole picture, including the role of natural supplements where appropriate.

Group medical visits

Time spent with patients together as well as one at a time, so that people learn from each other.

Her approach

The mind and the body in the same appointment

Dr. Desai treats mental and physical health together rather than sending them to separate rooms. Her clinical interests, as listed by her practice, are integrative psychiatry, mental health, natural supplements and specialty testing.
